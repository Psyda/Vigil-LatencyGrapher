Method: small HTTPS requests timed once per second against multiple independent endpoints over kept-alive connections. Endpoints are tested at startup and only working ones are used. This tracks round-trip latency closely. Absolute values read slightly higher than a raw ping, so compare results between households rather than against ping numbers. Spikes, jitter, and timeouts are directly comparable. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. All data stays on this device until you choose to send it.
